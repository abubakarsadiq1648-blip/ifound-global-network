Research & Innovation

Questions create discovery.
Evidence creates knowledge.

IFOUND explores scientific, technological and knowledge-based questions across multiple disciplines, with a long-term goal of making useful research more accessible and understandable.

Research interests can develop over time. The following areas represent IFOUND's long-term research and innovation directions.

01

Astronomy & Space

Exploring stars, planets, galaxies, cosmic phenomena, space science and humanity's understanding of the universe.

02

Earth & Geology

Research interests involving rocks, minerals, geological processes, Earth's history and the physical structure of our planet.

03

Biology & Life Sciences

Exploring living systems, biological processes, ecosystems and the complexity of life.

04

Human Health

Future research and technology directions related to health knowledge, scientific education and responsible information.

05

Agriculture

Exploring technology and scientific knowledge that could contribute to agricultural productivity and sustainability.

06

Environment & Climate

Understanding environmental systems, climate processes, sustainability and human interaction with the environment.

07

Artificial Intelligence

Researching intelligent systems, machine learning, knowledge systems and responsible AI applications.

08

Engineering & Robotics

Exploring mechanical systems, automation, robotics and technologies that connect software with the physical world.

09

Education & Knowledge

Developing ways technology can make complex information easier to discover, understand and learn.

10

Islamic Knowledge

Exploring technology-assisted approaches to Islamic and Qur'anic knowledge while respecting reliable sources and scholarly context.

Research Principle
“Faith inspires questions; evidence guides scientific conclusions.”

IFOUND can explore questions motivated by curiosity, philosophy or faith, while scientific conclusions must remain grounded in evidence and responsible research methods.

Research Method

Curiosity with responsibility.

01 — Question

Identify a meaningful question or problem.

02 — Evidence

Gather reliable information and observations.

03 — Analysis

Evaluate evidence carefully and transparently.

04 — Conclusion

Communicate conclusions according to the available evidence.
