OUR STORY

Building beyond a single application.

IFOUND is designed as a broader technology, research and knowledge organization — not a company limited to one product or one industry.

Why IFOUND exists

The world contains an enormous amount of information, technology and scientific knowledge, but finding, understanding and applying that information can still be difficult.

IFOUND aims to explore technology that can make useful information easier to discover, understand and use.

This begins with practical digital applications and expands toward artificial intelligence, scientific research, engineering and future technologies.

Develop technology and research platforms that make useful information, discovery and practical solutions easier to access and understand.

We believe technology should not simply become more advanced. It should become more useful, responsible, accessible and meaningful to the people who use it.

IFOUND RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

“Faith inspires questions; evidence guides scientific conclusions.”

Curiosity can inspire important questions about the world. Scientific conclusions, however, should be guided by appropriate evidence, research methods, testing and honest communication of uncertainty.
