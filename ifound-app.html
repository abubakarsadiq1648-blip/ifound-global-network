The Platform

A digital connection between people, products, businesses and location.

Instead of visiting many shops, calling different businesses, or asking several people where an item is available, users can search through IFOUND and discover relevant businesses based on location and available information.

For People

IFOUND is designed to help people answer a simple question: “Where can I find what I need?” Users can search for products, discover relevant businesses, examine available information and find the physical location.

For Businesses

IFOUND is also designed to answer: “How can people who need my products discover my shop?” Businesses can build a digital presence containing shop and product information so potential customers can discover them.

Location Intelligence

Start nearby. Explore farther when necessary.

IFOUND is designed around the idea that the user's current GPS position should become important when answering: “Where is the nearest place where I can find this?”

Nearby Search

The planned nearby-search experience can prioritize qualifying shops within a 10 km radius. If nothing relevant is found, the user can choose to explore farther.

Explore Search

No nearby result should not mean the end of the search.

If there are currently no matching shops within the nearby search radius, IFOUND can provide an option to search farther away.

Trust & Verification

A discoverable business should also be a trustworthy business.

IFOUND can use a staged verification workflow before a shop becomes publicly searchable. This is intended to reduce fake businesses, incorrect information, duplicate listings and inactive businesses appearing as active.

Submitted Shop information has been submitted.
Under Verification Information is being reviewed.
Approved The shop has passed the relevant review.
Live The business can become discoverable.
Suspended Visibility can be restricted when necessary.
Deactivated The listing can be removed from active discovery.

Scalable Architecture

Built to grow beyond a small collection of shops.

A global platform cannot depend on downloading a huge collection of businesses to every device and searching everything locally. The long-term architecture is intended to move toward dedicated search and geographic services.
