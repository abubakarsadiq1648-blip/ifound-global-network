Who I Am

Abubakar Sadiq Idriss is the founder and CEO of IFOUND, a technology and innovation organization focused on digital products, research, knowledge and future technology.

His interests include website development, programming, digital product development, scientific research, technology, entrepreneurship, creative design and photography.

Why IFOUND Exists

IFOUND was created with a broader purpose than building a single application. The organization is intended to grow into a technology, research and knowledge ecosystem.

The long-term goal is to create useful technologies while exploring questions across science, engineering, artificial intelligence, education and knowledge.
